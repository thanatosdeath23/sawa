How It Works

Get help or give help in three simple steps.

1

Post Your Need or Offer

Create a post in the right category — blood, medical, housing, food, power, or anything else — and describe what you need or can provide.

2

Browse & Connect

Search posts near you and contact the person directly — no middleman, no delay. Real help from real people nearby.

3

Mark It Resolved

Once help has been given or received, mark the post as resolved so the community knows and the board stays current.
